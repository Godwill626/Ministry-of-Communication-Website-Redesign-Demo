Responsibilities

Explore the core responsibilities of the Ministry of Communication, which underpin its mission to inform, educate, and connect Cameroon's citizens.

Core Responsibilities

  • Policy Formulation and Implementation: Developing and executing the government's communication strategy to foster transparency and citizen engagement.
  • Media Regulation: Overseeing media operations, ensuring ethical practices, and fostering media pluralism in the country.
  • Public Relations: Managing government communication, promoting Cameroon's image, and supporting other ministries' communication efforts.
  • Media Development: Contributing to media literacy, citizen awareness, and the growth of the media sector, including private and community media.
  • International Cooperation: Collaborating with international organizations and foreign governments on communication matters to enhance Cameroon's global standing.
  • Tutelage: Supervising public media institutions like the Cameroon Radio Television (CRTV) and the Cameroon Press and Editions Company (SOPECAM).
  • Media Monitoring and Research: Observing media trends, conducting public opinion polls, and providing data-driven insights for effective communication strategies.
