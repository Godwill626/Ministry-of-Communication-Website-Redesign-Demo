Ministry of Communication

The ministry is tasked with developing and implementing the government's communication strategy, regulating the media, promoting the country's image, and supporting other ministries in their communication efforts.

Who We Are

The Ministry of Communication of Cameroon is dedicated to fostering transparency, promoting national cohesion, and ensuring that citizens are informed about government policies and programs.

Our mission is to establish Cameroon as a global leader in communication and media excellence, empowering citizens and enhancing national development.
