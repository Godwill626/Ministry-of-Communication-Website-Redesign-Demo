Key Departments and Their Responsibilities

  • Secretariat General: Provides administrative and logistical support to the minister, coordinates the ministry's activities, and ensures the implementation of its policies.
  • Division of Legal Affairs: Handles legal matters, including drafting regulations, providing legal advice, and managing legal disputes.
  • Division of Studies, Planning, and Cooperation: Focuses on research, planning, and international cooperation in the field of communication.
  • Observatory of Media and Public Opinion: Monitors media content, conducts public opinion polls, and analyzes media trends.
  • Directorate of Internal Public Communication: Oversees government communication strategies, coordinates with other ministries, and manages media relations.
  • Directorate of International Communication and Image Promotion: Handles international communication, promotes Cameroon's image abroad, and coordinates with foreign governments and organizations.
  • Directorate of Development of Private Media and Advertising: Regulates private media and advertising agencies, and promotes media pluralism.
